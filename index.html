The Pitman Schedule

How it works: The Pitman Schedule is a fixed 12-hour shift rotation that follows a 28-day continuous cycle.

Key Benefits:

  • ⏱️ More Free Time: You work roughly 15 days a month (around 182 days a year compared to 260 in a standard 9-to-5 job).
  • 🗓️ Predictability: The fixed rotation allows you to plan your personal life, holidays, and vacations months or even years in advance.
  • 🏖️ Guaranteed Weekends Off: Every two weeks, you get a full 3-day weekend (Friday, Saturday, and Sunday) to rest and recharge.
  • ♻️ Fair Distribution: All teams share the exact same workload, weekends, and public holidays over time.
